02 — TREATMENT TECHNOLOGY

The equipment behind a compliant discharge point.

Every process below is deployed as part of a single engineered train — sized to your flow, not sold from a catalogue.

MBR

Membrane Bioreactor

Membrane separation replaces secondary sedimentation, intercepting microorganisms completely for a long sludge age and high MLSS. Effluent turbidity and suspended solids near zero — suitable for direct reuse.

OFFICES · CAR WASHES · TEXTILES · PAPER · BREWING

DAF

Dissolved Air Flotation

Micro-fine bubbles adhere to suspended matter and lift it for skimming. Strong on low-temperature, low-turbidity and algae-heavy water.

ALSO: VORTEX CONCAVE · VERTICAL FLOW

SHALLOW AF

High-Efficiency Shallow Flotation

Built on shallow pool theory (600–700 mm) and the zero-speed principle, giving static-state particle rise and suspended solids removal above 90%.

SS REMOVAL > 90%

CLARIFIERS

Intelligent Sedimentation

Slant-pipe lamella units at 60° shorten settling distance for a small footprint. Vertical flow units solve one-stop gravity separation at lower structural cost.

LAMINAR FLOW · 60° INCLINED PLATES

SLUDGE

Multi-Plate Screw Press

A screw shaft through fixed and moving rings carries sludge from gravity thickening into high-pressure dewatering. Continuous self-cleaning removes clogging risk and the separate thickening tank.

NON-CLOGGING · NO THICKENING TANK

DEWATERING

Plate & Frame Filter Press

Alternating solid plates and hollow frames compress filter cloth under manual or hydraulic locking, fully enclosed, producing a cake with markedly higher solid content.

ENCLOSED · HYDRAULIC LOCKING

POLISHING

Quartz Sand & Activated Carbon

Multi-grade compacted quartz beds combine membrane filtration, osmosis and contact coagulation. Carbon beds strip odour, colour, residual chlorine and dissolved heavy metals including hexavalent chromium and mercury.

07 — FAQ

Questions engineers and facility managers ask first.

How do you ensure compliance with DENR and LLDA regulations?+

All wastewater streams undergo certified treatment prior to discharge, satisfying the Philippine Clean Water Act of 2004. Systems are engineered to consistently hit and exceed the water quality guidelines and General Effluent Standards set by the DENR.

What industries do you serve?+

Commercial and hospitality (hotels and resorts), heavy industrial plants including textiles and refineries, high-density residential developments, healthcare institutions, and agro-industrial livestock facilities.

Do you provide end-to-end project management?+

Yes. We handle the entire cycle: initial DENR and LLDA environmental consultancy, structural civil works, advanced hardware integration, and managed vacuum tanker fleet hauling logistics.

What are the key advantages of your treatment technology?+

High-efficiency biological treatment removes BOD, COD and ammonia nitrogen within a single low-consumption structure. Small footprints, short construction windows, and automated operation requiring no permanent manual labour.

Is imported hardware compatible with local infrastructure?+

We handle the full technical bridge — all imported heavy machinery from our global partners is custom-configured to run on domestic Philippine 60Hz electrical grid structures and local civil engineering standards.

Do you offer direct pipeline connection for offices and pantries?+

Yes. Our units support direct pipeline connections for continuous water supply without manual tank refilling.

Are filter replacements available, and how often are they needed?+

We maintain a complete inventory of RO membranes, PAC composite filters and UF cartridges with dedicated technical support. Composite pre-filters are typically replaced every 6 to 12 months; main RO membranes last 12 to 24 months.
